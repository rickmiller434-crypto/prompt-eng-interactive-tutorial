# Business Case — Acme Onboarding Revamp

## Problem

30-day drop-off has risen from 19% to 28% over six quarters. CS hr per onboarding has risen 33% in the same period. The drop-off cohort represents ~$2.1M of annualized ARR at risk per quarter.

## Strategic alignment

FY26 strategic objective #2 (Land-and-Expand): improve mid-market net retention by 3 points. Current trajectory misses by 4–6 points; this project is the largest single contributor on the roadmap.

## Value hypothesis

**If we** redesign onboarding to deliver first-value in under a week with in-product guidance, **then we will** reduce 30-day drop-off below 15% and free CS time for expansion conversations, **as measured by** TTFV, drop-off rate, and CS-hours-per-onboarding, with secondary measure of 365d cohort expansion revenue.

## Benefits register

| # | Benefit | Owner | Measure | Baseline | Target | Realization |
|---|---------|-------|---------|----------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Faster TTFV | VP CS (Priya) | Median TTFV (days) | 14 | ≤ 5 | 60d post-launch |
| 2 | Lower drop-off | VP CS | 30-day drop-off rate | 28% | < 15% | 90d post-launch |
| 3 | CS hours reduced | Head of CS Ops | CS hr / onboarding | 6.4 | ≤ 2.0 | 60d post-launch |
| 4 | Expansion uplift | VP Sales | 365d cohort expansion revenue Δ | (cohort baseline) | +15% | 365d post-launch |

## Options considered

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons | Cost | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | | Cheapest | Drop-off worsens; FY26 retention misses plan | $0 | -$8M+ exposure |
| Incremental CS staffing | Hire 4 more CSMs | Fast | Doesn't fix root cause; CS cost rises | $560k/yr | Marginal |
| **Onboarding revamp (chosen)** | Redesign flow + in-product guidance + compliance gate | Treats root cause; reduces CS load; satisfies regulator | Cross-team coordination cost | $480k one-time | $2–4M retained ARR + expansion uplift |

## Recommendation

**Onboarding revamp.** It addresses the root cause (the experience), not the symptom (CS workload). The compliance overlay we'd need anyway by 1 Sep 2026 is absorbed into this work, avoiding a duplicate effort later. Sensitivity: TTFV target of ≤ 5 days is the linchpin — if we land at 7 days we still capture ~60% of benefit; below 9 days and the case weakens materially.

## Financial summary

- Cost (P50): $480k (P85: $540k)
- Benefit (P50, year 1): $2.1M retained ARR + $0.4M CS cost reduction
- Payback period: < 4 months post-launch
- Sensitivity: TTFV must land ≤ 7 days for positive case; ≤ 5 days for full case.

## Risks to the business case

| # | Risk | Impact if realized |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CS team resists role change; new flow under-utilized | Drop-off improvement < 50% of target |
| 2 | Compliance deadline overruns; gating delays GA past summer freeze | Benefit realization slides 1–2 quarters |
| 3 | Mid-market segment shifts (e.g., acquisition pause) | Cohort sizes too small to measure cleanly |

**Principles Honored:** 4 (value), 1 (stewardship), 5 (systems thinking), 10 (risk).
