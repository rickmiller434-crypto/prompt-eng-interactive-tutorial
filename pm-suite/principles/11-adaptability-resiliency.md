# Principle 11 — Adaptability & Resiliency

> Embrace adaptability and resiliency.

## Intent

Adaptability = the ability to respond to changing conditions. Resiliency = the ability to absorb disruption and recover. Both are designed into the project, not improvised after a shock.

## What it means in practice

- Plans assume change; rolling-wave / progressive elaboration is the default for anything beyond the near horizon.
- Buffers (schedule, budget, scope) are explicit, not hidden.
- Single points of failure (key person, single vendor, single tool) are identified and addressed.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-planning` produces plans with explicit buffers and a near-horizon detail / far-horizon outline structure.
- `pm-team` flags key-person dependencies and recommends knowledge-sharing actions.
- `pm-uncertainty` runs a periodic resilience review (what would break us, and how would we recover?).

## Audit checks

- [ ] Plan structure shows progressive elaboration (detail near, outline far).
- [ ] Schedule/budget buffers are explicit and rationalized.
- [ ] Single points of failure are identified with mitigation actions.
- [ ] Resilience review performed at least once per phase / quarter.
