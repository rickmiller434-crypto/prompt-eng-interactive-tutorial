# Principle 12 — Change

> Enable change to achieve the envisioned future state.

## Intent

Projects exist to move an organization (or product, or community) from a current state to a desired future state. Delivering outputs without enabling the human and organizational change required to use them produces no value.

## What it means in practice

- Organizational change management (OCM) is part of the project, not a separate effort that happens "later."
- Adoption is measured, not assumed.
- Training, communication, and support transitions are planned for and resourced.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-stakeholders` and `pm-delivery` jointly produce an **OCM Plan** when the project changes how people work.
- Adoption metrics appear in the measurement plan alongside output metrics.
- The closure phase includes an explicit handover to operations / sustainment.

## Audit checks

- [ ] OCM plan exists when the project changes user behavior or operating processes.
- [ ] Adoption metrics are defined and tracked.
- [ ] Training and support transitions are planned and resourced.
- [ ] Handover to sustainment is part of closure, not an afterthought.
