# Principle 3 — Stakeholders

> Effectively engage with stakeholders.

## Intent

Stakeholders are anyone who affects, is affected by, or perceives themselves to be affected by the project. Engagement is continuous, not a single workshop.

## What it means in practice

- Stakeholders are identified early and re-identified throughout the project (people, power, interest, and influence change).
- Engagement is tailored — a regulator, a sponsor, and an end-user need different cadences and content.
- Voice-of-the-stakeholder informs scope, value definition, and acceptance criteria.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-stakeholders` maintains a living **Stakeholder Register** and **Engagement Matrix** (current vs. desired engagement level).
- Any scope or acceptance criteria artifact must cite the stakeholder(s) who source the requirement.
- Communication plan is generated from the engagement matrix, not invented separately.

## Audit checks

- [ ] Stakeholder register exists and was updated within the last reporting period.
- [ ] Engagement matrix shows current and desired levels per stakeholder/group.
- [ ] Communications plan derives from the engagement matrix.
- [ ] Requirements and acceptance criteria trace to named stakeholders.
