# Principle 1 — Stewardship

> Be a diligent, respectful, and caring steward.

## Intent

Project managers act as stewards of the organization, the team, the customers, society, and the environment. Stewardship covers integrity, care, trustworthiness, and compliance.

## What it means in practice

- Decisions are made transparently and recorded.
- Conflicts of interest are surfaced, not hidden.
- Regulatory, contractual, ethical, sustainability, and safety obligations are explicit, not implicit.
- The team is treated as people, not throughput.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-lead` requires every recorded decision to name the steward (owner) and the people/groups affected.
- All artifacts include an `Obligations & Constraints` section when relevant (regulatory, contractual, ethical).
- `pm-team` enforces sustainable pace in its plans (no agent will silently approve schedules that require unflagged overtime).

## Audit checks

- [ ] Decisions log present and current; each entry names a steward and impacted parties.
- [ ] Regulatory/contractual obligations identified in charter and referenced in plans.
- [ ] Schedule and resource plans do not contain hidden overcommitment.
- [ ] Sensitive data handling is documented when applicable.
