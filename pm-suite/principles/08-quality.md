# Principle 8 — Quality

> Build quality into processes and deliverables.

## Intent

Quality is not an inspection at the end. It is designed into the work, verified continuously, and owned by everyone on the team. Quality applies to processes, intermediate artifacts, and final deliverables.

## What it means in practice

- Acceptance criteria are defined **before** work starts, not after.
- "Definition of Done" is shared, written, and enforced.
- Rework is a signal, not an embarrassment — its sources are analyzed.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-delivery` will not mark an item complete without acceptance criteria having been pre-recorded and verified.
- `pm-measurement` tracks rework rate, defect escape rate, and process metrics relevant to the lifecycle.
- All artifacts have their own quality checklist (see `audit checks` in each principle and template).

## Audit checks

- [ ] Definition of Done / acceptance criteria are written before execution begins.
- [ ] Quality metrics are part of the measurement plan.
- [ ] Rework / defects are tracked and reviewed in retros / phase reviews.
- [ ] Process improvements from retros are actually implemented (closed loop).
