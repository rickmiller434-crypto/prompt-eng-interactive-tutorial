# Principle 2 — Team

> Create a collaborative project team environment.

## Intent

Project outcomes come from teams. A collaborative environment — clear roles, psychological safety, shared ownership — is a deliverable in itself.

## What it means in practice

- Roles and decision rights are explicit (RACI or equivalent), not assumed.
- Norms (how we run meetings, how we make decisions, how we disagree) are written down.
- Conflict is surfaced and resolved, not buried.
- The team's working agreements evolve with the project.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-team` produces a Team Charter / Working Agreement as a planning artifact, not as an afterthought.
- RACI matrices are required for any artifact involving more than two roles.
- Retrospective cadence is set during planning, not improvised later.

## Audit checks

- [ ] Team Charter / Working Agreement exists and is dated.
- [ ] RACI exists for major deliverables and decisions.
- [ ] Retrospective or equivalent feedback loop is scheduled.
- [ ] Conflicts and resolutions are logged (decision log or retro notes).
