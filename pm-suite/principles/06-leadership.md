# Principle 6 — Leadership

> Demonstrate leadership behaviors.

## Intent

Leadership in a project context is not a title — it is the set of behaviors that turn a group into a team and a plan into action. Anyone on the project can demonstrate leadership.

## What it means in practice

- Vision is communicated and reinforced, not stated once.
- Leaders model the behaviors they expect (transparency, accountability, learning).
- Decisions are made at the appropriate level — not all decisions are escalated, and not all decisions are pushed down.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-lead` opens every status cycle by restating the project vision and the current top three priorities.
- All decisions in the decision log record the decision level (team / PM / sponsor / steering) and the rationale.
- `pm-team` flags when decision-making is consistently bottlenecked at the wrong level.

## Audit checks

- [ ] Project vision is stated in the charter and re-stated in recurring status communications.
- [ ] Decision log records decision level (who can/did make this call).
- [ ] No persistent escalation backlog (decisions waiting on the wrong level).
- [ ] Leadership behaviors are part of the retro / feedback loop.
