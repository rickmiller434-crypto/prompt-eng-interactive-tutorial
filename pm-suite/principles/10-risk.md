# Principle 10 — Risk

> Optimize risk responses.

## Intent

Risk is uncertainty that matters. Projects optimize — not minimize — risk responses, because eliminating all risk is impossible and chasing low-impact risks wastes resources. Opportunities are risks too.

## What it means in practice

- Risks are categorized (threat / opportunity), assessed (probability × impact, or distribution where appropriate), owned, and responded to.
- Response strategies are deliberate: avoid / mitigate / transfer / accept (threat); exploit / enhance / share / accept (opportunity).
- Risk attitude (appetite, tolerance, threshold) is documented at the project and stakeholder level.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-uncertainty` owns the **Risk Register**, with response strategy, owner, trigger, and review cadence per item.
- High-impact risks have a documented response plan, not just a status.
- Opportunities are tracked alongside threats — the register is not threat-only.

## Audit checks

- [ ] Risk register exists with owner, probability, impact, response, trigger per item.
- [ ] Risk register includes opportunities, not only threats.
- [ ] Risk appetite / tolerance documented for the project.
- [ ] Top risks reviewed each reporting cycle; aged risks revisited or closed.
