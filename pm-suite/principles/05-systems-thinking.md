# Principle 5 — Systems Thinking

> Recognize, evaluate, and respond to system interactions.

## Intent

Projects are open systems. They influence and are influenced by other projects, programs, portfolios, the organization, and the wider environment. Local optimization that ignores system interactions destroys value.

## What it means in practice

- Dependencies on and from other initiatives are mapped.
- Second-order effects of decisions are considered (a faster schedule may overload shared services; cutting scope X may break another team's roadmap).
- Feedback loops within the project (planning → execution → measurement → re-planning) are intentional.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-approach` and `pm-planning` produce a **Dependency Map** showing inbound and outbound dependencies on the system.
- `pm-uncertainty` considers systemic risks (shared resources, organizational change, market shifts), not only project-local risks.
- Decisions that affect other teams or systems require a notification entry in the comms plan.

## Audit checks

- [ ] Dependency map exists and shows direction (inbound / outbound).
- [ ] Risk register includes at least one systemic / external risk.
- [ ] Decision log shows consideration of cross-system impact for material decisions.
- [ ] Comms plan reaches affected adjacent teams, not only direct stakeholders.
