# Principle 9 — Complexity

> Navigate complexity.

## Intent

Complexity arises from human behavior, system behavior, ambiguity, and emergence. The PM's job is not to eliminate complexity (often impossible) but to recognize it and respond appropriately.

## What it means in practice

- The project's complexity profile is assessed, not assumed. (Cynefin or equivalent: clear / complicated / complex / chaotic.)
- The response is matched to the complexity: best-practice for clear, expert analysis for complicated, **probe-sense-respond** for complex, **act-sense-respond** for chaotic.
- Complexity assessments are revisited as the project evolves.

## Agent behaviors

- `pm-approach` produces a **Complexity Assessment** as part of tailoring; it informs lifecycle and cadence choice.
- `pm-uncertainty` distinguishes risks (knowable) from emergent uncertainty (requires sensing, not planning).
- `pm-lead` resists premature certainty — pushes for experimentation in complex domains.

## Audit checks

- [ ] Complexity assessment exists and is referenced by tailoring decisions.
- [ ] Response strategies are mapped to the complexity profile.
- [ ] Project has explicit mechanisms for sensing (retros, customer feedback, telemetry) in complex domains.
- [ ] Assessment is revisited at phase gates / major increments.
