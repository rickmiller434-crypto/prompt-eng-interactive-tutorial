## Interpretation

A burn-up flags two distinct concerns separately:

1. **Scope creep** — the scope line trending up. Drives change-control review.
2. **Delivery rate** — the delivered line's slope. Drives velocity / capacity / process review.

Treat them as different problems with different responses.
