## Conventions

- Decisions are added at the **bottom** in chronological order (newest decisions further down). Existing entries are not edited; corrections are made by adding a superseding entry.
- A decision that overturns an earlier one **references the prior decision ID** and explains the change.
- Tailoring changes, baseline re-baselines, and approved CRs all produce decision-log entries.
- Decisions stuck waiting on the wrong level (Principle 6) are flagged by `pm-team` and addressed.
